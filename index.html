01 — ABOUT THE TRUST

Education should open doors—not define limits.

Riyachat Educational Trust is a proposed educational initiative built around a simple belief: quality learning can help transform individual lives and strengthen communities.

Our focus is on creating opportunities for children to develop academic ability, character, communication, creativity and the confidence to contribute to society.

A long-term vision for meaningful change.
02 — OUR VISION

03 — FLAGSHIP PROJECT

A PROPOSED SCHOOL FOR KAILASHAHAR

APEX PUBLIC SCHOOL

Kailashahar · Unakoti · Tripura

PLANNED
2028
RISE BEYOND LIMITS.

A school where every strength has room to grow.

Apex Public School is a proposed co-educational English-medium school project envisioned for Kailashahar, Tripura. The initial plan is to serve learners from Play School through Class VIII, with a balanced focus on academic foundations and all-round development.

The project is in its planning and development stage. Details may evolve as the initiative progresses and the necessary approvals and resources are secured.
